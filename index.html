About Me

I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Houston focused on machine learning, responsible AI, and linguistic diversity. My work explores equitable AI systems for under‑represented languages, combining deep learning with socio‑technical perspectives. I enjoy translating research insights into deployable solutions and engaging with interdisciplinary teams to drive impact.
